# SyncMesh — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 3 July 2026_

SyncMesh ("the app") is designed to be private by default. This policy explains what the
app does and does not do with your information.

## Summary
SyncMesh does not collect, transmit to us, or sell any personal data. There is no account,
no cloud server, and no analytics. All syncing happens directly between your own devices on
your local network.

## Data the app handles (only on your devices)
- **Clipboard content** you copy is sent directly to the other devices you have paired, over
  your local Wi-Fi network, and stored in a local history database on each device.
- **Files** you choose to send are transferred directly to the paired device you select and
  saved to that device's storage (Downloads/SyncMesh or a folder you choose).
- **Device information** such as a device name (e.g. manufacturer and model) and a randomly
  generated device ID and pairing code are exchanged between paired devices so they can find
  and trust each other on the local network.

This information never leaves your local network and is never sent to the developer or any
third party. It is stored only on your own devices and can be deleted at any time by clearing
history or uninstalling the app.

## Data we collect
None. The developer receives no data from the app. There are no analytics, advertising, or
tracking SDKs.

## Permissions and why they are used
- **Local network / Wi-Fi state:** to discover and connect to your other devices on the same
  network.
- **Notifications:** to show the sync service status and incoming file-transfer requests.
- **Camera:** only when you choose to scan a pairing QR code. No images are stored or sent.
- **Foreground service:** to keep clipboard sync and transfers running reliably.

## Security
Connections are made directly between devices you pair on your own local network. SyncMesh is
intended for use on networks you trust (your home or personal Wi-Fi/hotspot). Data is not
transmitted over the internet by the app.

## Children's privacy
SyncMesh is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.
