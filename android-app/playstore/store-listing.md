## Full description  [4000 max]
```
SyncMesh links the devices around you into a private local mesh — so your clipboard and your files move between them instantly, over your own Wi-Fi, with no cloud and no account.

Copy a link on your phone and paste it on your tablet. Send a photo, a video, a document or an APK to another device in seconds. Everything travels directly, device to device, and never leaves your local network.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
WHAT YOU CAN DO
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

• CLIPBOARD SYNC — Copy on one device, paste on another automatically. SyncMesh keeps a searchable history of everything you copy, and you can re-copy any past item with a tap.

• FILE TRANSFER — Send single or multiple files up to 2 GB each. Watch live progress with transfer speed and ETA, and pause, resume or cancel anytime. Received files are saved to Downloads/SyncMesh, or a folder you choose.

• EASY PAIRING — Connect two devices in one tap: scan a QR code, pick a nearby device, or type a 6-digit code. Pair once, sync forever.

• BUNDLED KEYBOARD — Android limits background clipboard access, so SyncMesh includes an optional keyboard that reliably sends your clipboard the moment you start typing.

• TRANSFER HISTORY — Review every file you've sent or received with status, size and duration.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PRIVATE BY DESIGN
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

• No account. No cloud. No analytics.
• Your clipboard and files never leave your local Wi-Fi network.
• History is stored only on your own device.
• Direct, peer-to-peer connections between devices you pair yourself.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
HOW IT WORKS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. Install SyncMesh on two or more devices on the same Wi-Fi.
2. Tap Start Sync on each device.
3. Pair them with a QR code, a nearby tap, or a 6-digit code.
4. Copy text or pick files — they appear on your other devices.

SyncMesh is perfect for anyone with more than one Android device who wants frictionless copy-paste and fast file sharing without uploading anything to the internet.

Note: SyncMesh works over a shared local network (Wi-Fi or hotspot). Devices must be on the same network to sync.
```
